Government Performance Unit

CIRCULAR No. 07/2027
Date: 10 January 2027

To: All Federal Ministries and Authorities

Subject: Submission of annual KPI results for 2026

All entities are required to upload their 2026 key performance indicator results, with supporting evidence, to the Government Performance System by 31 March 2027. Results must follow the updated KPI dictionary (version 3.2) attached to this circular.

Entities that do not submit on time will be marked as non-compliant in the annual excellence assessment.
