National Cybersecurity Centre – Demo
URGENT – SECURITY ADVISORY

Ref: NCSC-ADV-2027-009
Date: 15 February 2027

To: CIO, Ministry of Digital Services – Demo

Subject: URGENT – Critical vulnerability in VPN gateways

A critical remote-code-execution vulnerability (CVE-2027-10442) is being actively exploited in "SecureGate" VPN appliances. Our monitoring indicates that two of your Ministry's public IP addresses run an affected version.

Required actions, immediately:
1. Apply vendor patch 9.4.2 or disable the affected portal.
2. Review VPN logs since 1 February 2027 for indicators of compromise (attached).
3. Confirm remediation to the NCSC within 48 hours.
